Join us

Join the Lab

We welcome students and researchers interested in trauma, psychiatric mental health nursing, and digital health interventions.

Graduate students

Master's & doctoral study

Graduate study is through the Chung-Ang University Red Cross College of Nursing. Students design and run their own studies within the lab's research themes, alongside team projects.

Undergraduate interns

Research internships

Nursing undergraduates can join as research interns and take part in literature reviews, data management, and the development of the lab's apps and simulations.

Collaborators

Researchers & partners

We work with clinicians, disaster mental-health practitioners, and researchers in other fields. Get in touch if your work connects with one of our themes.

Openings depend on the lab's current projects and funding, so please contact us before you apply.
